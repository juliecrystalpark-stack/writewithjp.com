Localization + content systems
Confidential game project

Giving promotions a naming system—not another one-off title.

From individually translated titles to a consistent taxonomy the team could reuse.

Explore the case study

The challenge: Similar promotions had different English titles, making the event type harder to recognize at a glance.

My contribution: I grouped labels around the underlying event category rather than translating each title in isolation. I also flagged a region-specific payment reference for review.

What the team gained: A reusable naming taxonomy for future promotions. Reduced recognition effort was the design goal; I do not have player research measuring that effect.

Notifications + tone strategy
Confidential game project

Saving urgency for the moments that actually need it.

Giving routine reminders and real deadlines different voices and jobs.

Explore the case study

The challenge: Notification triggers with different stakes sounded too similar.

My contribution: I matched tone to each trigger’s actual urgency and reviewed repeated phrasing across the set.

What the team gained: A more consistent approach to notification tone, with routine reminders distinguished from time-sensitive messages.

Missions + UI microcopy
Confidential game project

Making mission requirements make sense to players.

Clear instructions start with understanding what the game actually asks the player to do.

Explore the case study

The challenge: Technical mission specifications needed to become understandable player-facing instructions.

My contribution: I rewrote conditions in concise, action-led language and asked the team to clarify ambiguous requirements instead of guessing.

What the team gained: Clearer instructions and a clarified win condition that QA could verify.

Tutorials + character voice
Confidential game project

A different narrator. The same clear next step.

Keeping instructional meaning intact while giving a seasonal character a distinct personality.

Explore the case study

The challenge: Change the narrator’s personality without changing what the tutorial teaches.

My contribution: I preserved each instructional beat and developed alternative lines around the character’s vocabulary, rhythm and attitude.

What the team gained: A repeatable approach to character-led tutorial adaptation that retained the underlying game logic.

Narrative + character development
Confidential narrative project

Building people, not stereotypes.

Research and interviews informing character profiles, voice and collaboration with development and audio.

Explore the case study

My contribution: I used interviews, research and real-life context to shape character profiles and collaborated with development and audio teams.
