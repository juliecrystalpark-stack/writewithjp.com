03 — Frameworks

How I think about copy

Two original frameworks I bring to every project — one for onboarding, one for error states.

AHH Onboarding

A gut-check for first sessions. Good onboarding copy should do three things, in this order.

A

Accessible

No jargon. No assuming the player knows what they're doing yet.

H

Hey You

Speaks to a real person, not a user. Warm and direct.

H

Hell Ya

Builds momentum. Leaves the player feeling capable.

OUCH Error States

A gut-check for every error message. Run any error screen through these four questions.

O

Own It

Say what happened and why. No blame, no vagueness.

U

Uplift

Reassure the player. Give context so they don't panic.

C

Clear Path

Tell them exactly what to do next. One step, right now.

H

Help Out

Always give a way out. Never leave players stranded.
