Services

Words that help players understand—and help teams stay aligned.

Onboarding, Tutorials & UI Microcopy

I turn mechanics and backend requirements into player-facing language that teaches through action and keeps momentum moving.

  • Buttons, tooltips, missions, rewards, errors, and progression messaging
  • Content hierarchy and UX writing recommendations
  • Developer-ready copy decks and implementation notes

Localization Editing for Western Markets

I edit translated English around player intent, interface context, market expectations, and repeatable terminology—not literal phrasing alone.

  • Naming systems and terminology frameworks
  • Cultural and regional relevance checks
  • Consistency reviews across connected flows

Voice, Tone & Content Systems

I turn an intended personality into practical guidance a whole team can apply consistently.

  • Voice and tone frameworks
  • Character voice adaptation
  • Notification and urgency systems
  • Examples, do/don’t guidance, and reusable templates
When characters need to feel human—not familiar

Character Profiles & Narrative Voice

I build research-led character foundations informed by interviews, real-life context, and careful attention to cultural specificity. The goal is emotional truth without stereotype.

  • Character profiles, motivations, relationships, and contradictions
  • Dialogue and behavioural voice guidance
  • Research synthesis and respectful representation checks
  • Cross-functional foundations for writing, development, sound, and music
When the game is live but the copy has never been reviewed as a system

UX Writing Audit

I identify where language creates confusion, inconsistency, unnecessary friction, or tonal mismatch—and turn the findings into prioritized recommendations.

  • Full product, feature, or single-flow audits
  • Annotated findings with suggested rewrites
  • Priority levels and recurring-pattern analysis
  • Optional follow-up implementation support

02 — Build

03 — Deliver

You receive usable copy plus the reasoning, systems, or documentation needed to keep it consistent.
